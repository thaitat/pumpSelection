Laterals carrying the sprinklers

Design rule: pressure loss along a lateral should not exceed 20% of head operating pressure, so first and last heads differ in flow by no more than about 10%.

      Formulas used

      Pipe friction, Hazen-Williams: hf = 10.67·L·Q^1.852 / (C^1.852·D^4.87) (Q in m³/s, D = actual inside diameter in m)

      Multiple-outlet lateral: F = 1/(m+1) + 1/(2N) + √(m−1)/(6N²) where m = 1.852

      Total head: TDH = static head + hf suction + hf main + ΣK·v²/2g + ¾ hf lateral + head pressure, plus margin

      Water power: P = ρ·g·Q·H. Shaft power = water power ÷ pump efficiency. 1 HP = 0.746 kW

      NPSHa = atmospheric head (by site elevation) − vapor pressure at 30°C (0.43 m) − suction lift − suction losses

      PVC sizes and wall thicknesses from Table 2 of Thai standard TIS 17-2561. All values are estimates for preliminary design; confirm against the actual pump curve before buying.
